# Ludify

*Disce ludendo* — learn by playing.

Give Ludify a topic, answer a few questions about what you want to know, and get back a short,
playable deck of cards with quick quizzes, guesses and decisions, every fact linked to its
source. The research runs inside Claude or ChatGPT, on your own account.

Early days: see [SPEC.md](SPEC.md) for the plan and [docs/evidence.md](docs/evidence.md) for the
research the design rests on.
